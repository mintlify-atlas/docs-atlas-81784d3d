Harvey knows how to collect 23 signals about a business. It collects **none of them** until you explicitly confirm which ones you want. On first run, `harvey signals` seeds the full catalog and shows everything in a proposed state — you then decide which signals are worth collecting for your ICP. This is not a skippable step: a fresh install prospects against nothing until at least one signal is confirmed.

---

## harvey signals

`harvey signals` with no flags displays the full catalog grouped by category, with each signal's current status (`confirmed`, `proposed`, or `rejected`), its cost note, and the number of companies already carrying that signal in your database.

With `--confirm` or `--reject`, it flips the status of one or more signals by code and exits.

**Syntax**

```bash
harvey signals [--confirm CODES] [--reject CODES]
```

**Flags**

<ParamField path="--confirm" type="string">
  Comma-separated signal codes to confirm. Two special values are accepted in addition to individual codes:
  - `all` — confirm every signal in the catalog
  - `free` — confirm every signal whose cost note begins with "free" or "included" (currently 21 of the 23 signals)
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="--reject" type="string">
  Comma-separated signal codes to turn off. Rejected signals are not collected on future runs. You can re-confirm them at any time.
</ParamField>

**Without flags — catalog review**

Running `harvey signals` alone prints the full catalog:

```
  Signals — 21 confirmed, 2 awaiting you, 0 off
  ==================================================================

  DISCOVERY
    [on]  SERP_RANK              Search rank (positions 11–30)
          $0.0018 / search (DataForSEO SERP)
    [on]  NO_WEBSITE             Has a listing but no site
          free (OpenStreetMap / DataForSEO Listings)
    [ ? ] UNCLAIMED_LISTING      Google Business Profile unclaimed
          free (DataForSEO Listings)
    …

  PROFILE
    [on]  INCUMBENT_AGENCY       Agency credited in footer
          free (HTTP profiler)
    …
```

Each row shows the signal code, a plain-language label, the current status mark (`[on]` / `[ ? ]` / `[off]`), and the cost note. When companies already carry the signal, a count is appended.

**Examples**

```bash
# Review the full catalog
harvey signals

# Confirm everything that costs nothing (21 signals)
harvey signals --confirm free

# Confirm a paid signal explicitly
harvey signals --confirm SERP_RANK

# Confirm multiple signals at once
harvey signals --confirm INCUMBENT_AGENCY,RUNNING_GOOGLE_ADS,NO_ONLINE_BOOKING

# Turn off a signal
harvey signals --reject LINKEDIN_CONNECT

# Turn off several signals
harvey signals --reject LINKEDIN_CONNECT,BLOCKS_AI_CRAWLERS
```

<Note>
Nothing is collected until a signal is confirmed. A fresh install prospects against nothing by design — you decide what a good prospect looks like before Harvey goes looking.
</Note>

<Tip>
Start with `harvey signals --confirm free` to confirm the 21 zero-cost signals in one command. You can then evaluate the two paid signals (`SERP_RANK` and any requiring an API key) separately with `--estimate` before committing to them.
</Tip>

The dashboard's **Signals** tab provides the same confirm/reject actions alongside signal descriptions, the cohort builder, and live company counts — useful for exploring what each signal actually means before committing to it.
